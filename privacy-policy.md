# Privacy Policy — Pramaan

**Effective date:** 2026-10-19

---

## 1. The short version

**Pramaan collects no personal data.** The application runs on your machine, uses the market data you give it, and does not transmit your data to us. There is no account, no analytics, and no telemetry enabled by default.

## 2. What the software accesses

| Data | Purpose | Leaves your device? |
|---|---|---|
| Market data (OHLCV) | Backtesting | No — fetched by *your* machine from *your* configured provider |
| Backtest parameters | Producing the result you asked for | No |
| Backtest results | Display and optional local export | No |
| Local configuration and cache | Persistence | No |

## 3. Data we do not collect

We do not collect, store, or process: your name, email address, phone number, IP address (beyond the unavoidable connection to your data provider), device identifiers, location, portfolio contents, portfolio values, holdings, or search history.

## 4. Optional services, if you enable them

If you opt in to an optional online feature (for example cloud sync of run history), the following applies:

- We collect the minimum necessary: an account identifier and the artifacts you choose to sync.
- Data is retained until you delete it.
- You may export and delete your data at any time, in full.
- Data at rest is encrypted; data in transit uses TLS.
- We will notify you of a breach within 72 hours of becoming aware of it.

Any such feature is opt-in and disabled by default.

## 5. Cookies and tracking

Pramaan does not use cookies for tracking. The web dashboard may set a strictly necessary local-storage entry to remember your preferences within your own browser only.

## 6. Your data provider

Market data is obtained from the data source you configure. Pramaan's relationship with that provider is between that provider and you — Pramaan is not a party to it and does not hold a copy of the data.

## 7. Children

Pramaan is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect data from them.

## 8. India — Digital Personal Data Act, 2023

Pramaan's default configuration processes no personal data as defined under the DPDP Act, 2023. If an optional online feature is enabled, we act as a data fiduciary with notice, purpose limitation, storage limitation, and erasure on request.

## 9. GDPR (non-EEA users)

If you access Pramaan from the EEA, the same minimal-scope principles apply: no personal data is processed by default, and any optional collection is on a lawful basis with a right to access, rectify, erase, and port your data.

## 10. Contact

For any privacy question, open an issue on the public repository. No support email is required because no personal data is held.
