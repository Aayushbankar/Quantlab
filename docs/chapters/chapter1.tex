\chapter{Introduction}
\section{The Retail Trading Landscape and SEBI Findings}
Recent empirical analyses published by the Securities and Exchange Board of India (SEBI) have uncovered a systemic crisis within retail derivative and equity trading. The data indicates a staggering statistic: over 93\% of retail participants incur net losses over consecutive trading horizons. These losses are primarily driven by acute behavioral biases, lack of robust risk management, and the exploitation of retail latency by institutional market makers.
\section{Project Rationale}
This project proposes a scientifically rigorous, event-driven backtesting and trading engine designed to level the playing field. By enforcing strict mathematical validation and market microstructure realism, the system mitigates the illusory profitability that plagues amateur algorithmic design.
